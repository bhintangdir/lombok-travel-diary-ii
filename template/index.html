FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers

How do I book a tour?

Tap any Book Now button to open WhatsApp, tell us your dates and group size, and we will send an itinerary and price.

Are the tours private?

Yes. All tours are private for your group, so you can set your own pace.

Do I need to pay in advance?

A small deposit secures your date and the balance is paid on the day. We will explain the payment options in chat.

Can you pick me up from my hotel, the airport or the harbour?

Of course. Hotel pickup is included in our tours, and we offer transfers from the airport and all main harbours.

What is the best time to visit Lombok?

May to September is dry and sunny, which is perfect for beaches and islands. The wet season is quieter and greener, and we adjust plans to the weather.
