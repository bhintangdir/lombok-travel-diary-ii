The Locals Behind Your Diary

We're a small team on Lombok who love sharing our island, one honest, friendly, unforgettable day at a time.

Our story

It Started with a Notebook

Lombok Travel Diary began with a simple habit: writing down the best beaches, warungs and hidden viewpoints we discovered as locals. Friends and visitors kept asking to see that notebook, so we turned it into tours.

Today we plan private trips for travellers from all over the world. No big buses and no rushed schedules. Just a local driver-guide, a flexible plan and the best pages of Lombok.

Everything is booked by chat. You talk to a real person, get clear prices and your plan is ready fast.
