FAQ

Before You Book

What is included in the tour price?

Each card lists what's included. Usually this is a private car with driver-guide, hotel pickup, fuel, parking and bottled water. Boat and entrance fees are stated per tour.

Can I change the itinerary?

Yes. Every tour is private, so we can swap stops, add a lunch spot or start later. Just tell us on WhatsApp.

How many people can join one tour?

Our standard cars fit up to 5–6 guests. Larger groups are welcome, and we will arrange extra vehicles.

What if the weather is bad?

We check sea and weather conditions the day before. If a boat trip is unsafe, we reschedule or swap to a land-based plan at no extra stress.

How do I pay?

A small deposit secures your date, and the balance is paid on the day. We will share the payment options in chat.
