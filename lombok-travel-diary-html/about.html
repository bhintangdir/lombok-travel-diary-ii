We are Lombok Travel Diary, and we exist to help you see Lombok the way we do: unhurried, honest and full of surprises.

Our story

Lombok Travel Diary started with a simple idea: visitors deserve a trip planned by people who actually live here. No call centres, no confusing packages, just a real conversation on WhatsApp.

Today we arrange tours, car and scooter rentals and airport transfers across Lombok and the Gili Islands. Every plan is shaped around you, whether you want a slow beach week or a volcano sunrise.
