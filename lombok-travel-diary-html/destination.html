Best Time to Visit Lombok

Understand Lombok's seasonal weather patterns to plan the ideal spots for your trip.

May to September

Dry Season & Clear Skies

The prime window for trekking Sembalun ridges (Pergasingan, Savana Dandaun, Anak Dara), snorkeling calm turquoise reefs in 3 Gilis & Secret Gilis, and catching iconic Merese sunsets.

April & October

Quiet Shoulder Season

Peaceful island atmosphere with fewer travellers and warm coastal breezes. Ideal for discovering secluded hidden gems like Semeti Beach, Tanjung Bloam, and remote Secret Gilis.

November to March

Green Season & Waterfalls

Lombok turns lush and emerald green. Rainforest waterfalls like Tiu Kelep, Sendang Gile, and Benang Kelambu reach their most dramatic and thunderous flow.
